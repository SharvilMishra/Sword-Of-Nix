# Sword Of Nix — Current Rewritten Story

This document is the canonical story reference for the version implemented in the game. Runtime dialogue is loaded from `data/story.xml`; fallback comic captions and gameplay cards are mirrored in their scene scripts.

## Current premise

**Shadow-041** is the forty-first repetition of the same night. Shadow has lived through the storm, the Akai rooftops, the five-floor hideout, the Warden fight and the cargo execution forty times. The cargo is Nix, the spirit bound to Shadow's sword, now living in a cat's body. The Akai gang does not know what she is; the Warden protects her. The client “N.” wants her dead because the sword only obeys while she lives. This time, after hearing her meow, Shadow hesitates and the loop's outcome is left unresolved.

Klint and Quel are the two operatives who have carried the Shadow codename. The story addresses either selected character as Shadow.

## Main story sequence

### 1. Encrypted phone briefing

Shown before the rooftop prologue. The sequence has six messages:

1. **Encrypted Contract** — Contract ID: Shadow-041. Time: 02:13 AM. Sender: N.
2. **Objective** — Destroy the target marked “cargo.” Do it properly this time.
3. **Location** — East District Power Station // Akai gang stronghold
4. **Intel Summary** — The cargo is held by the criminal organization “Akai gang.” Break through the outer strongholds first. The Warden will be waiting. He has always been waiting.
5. **Payment** — Paid upon confirmed destruction. Previous payments: uncollected.
6. **Instructions** — Don't ask what the cargo is. Leave no witnesses. Don't hesitate.

### 2. Akai gang outer rooftops

During a thunderstorm, Shadow materializes in a lightning strike. An Akai guard challenges him. Shadow's unnervingly accurate one-liner suggests that he has heard this conversation before. Combat begins, a gunner raises the alarm, and reinforcements arrive.

**Lightning arrival**

- **Akai Blade Guard:** What the hell?! Did you just come out of the lightning?!

**First confrontation**

- **Akai Blade Guard:** Who are you? You don't walk in here like you've been invited. Leave before I lose my temper.
- **Shadow:** Next you'll say I'm looking for trouble.
- **Akai Blade Guard:** Looking for... how did you know that? Fine. Trouble it is.

**Alarm**

- **Akai Gunner:** Over here!! Somebody's on the roof! We're under attack!

**Reinforcements**

- **Akai reinforcements:** Take him down!!

**Prologue completion card**

- **Breach Complete** — Akai Gang Perimeter Breached
- Supporting line: Forty times before. The same rain. The same lightning. Shadow moves deeper into the stronghold.

### 3. Akai hideout

Shadow climbs five floors through the stronghold. The guards are unaware of the loop, but their casual talk reveals a creature in the warehouse that they fear to approach and a Warden who never leaves his post. Clearing the top floor opens the route to the Warden.

**Floor 1 — the rain and the intruder**

- **Akai Blade Guard:** When is this rain going to stop? My boots are soaked through.
- **Akai Gunner:** Quit whining. We'll grab a drink upstairs after this shift.
- **Akai Blade Guard:** Wait. Someone's over there.

**Floor 2 — something in the warehouse**

- **Akai Blade Guard:** That thing in the warehouse is scratching the crate again.
- **Akai Gunner:** Don't go near it. The boss said the Warden feeds it himself.
- **Akai Blade Guard:** It's gone quiet downstairs. Intruder!

**Floor 3 — the Warden**

- **Akai Gunner:** Is the Warden still upstairs?
- **Akai Blade Guard:** He hasn't moved from his post in three days. Nobody gets past him.
- **Akai Gunner:** Somebody's about to. Open fire!

**Floor 4 — alarms and pursuit**

- **Akai Gunner:** Why are the alarms downstairs all lit up?
- **Akai Blade Guard:** Hold the stairs. Whatever it takes, he doesn't reach the top floor.
- **Akai Gunner:** Got him! Under the platform!

**Floor 5 — the cargo**

- **Akai Blade Guard:** Isn't the Warden coming down to help?
- **Akai Gunner:** He won't leave. His orders are to guard the cargo, and he takes those orders personally.
- **Akai Gunner:** Target on the top floor! Crossfire!

**Hideout completion card**

- Stronghold Breach // The Warden Is Upstairs

### 4. Warden battle

The Warden is protecting Nix, not guarding loot. He half-remembers this repeated night and is the first person to recognize Shadow across the loop.

**Arrival**

- **The Warden:** Stop. You break into someone's home and cut down his people. Don't you think that's rude?
- **Shadow:** Yes. It is.
- **The Warden:** Hm. Honest, at least. Odd. I feel like I've heard that answer before.
- **Shadow:** You have.
- **The Warden:** Fine. Let's see how honest you stay after I tear off both your legs.

**After the Warden is beaten**

- **The Warden:** You know my moves. Every one of them. Like you've bled on this floor before.
- **Shadow:** Forty times.
- **The Warden:** Forty... That's it, isn't it? You're not here for the Akai. You're here to...

The sentence ends there. Chapter two can finish it: “...kill her. Again.”

### 5. Cargo reveal — Act II

The post-boss comic returns to the warehouse where forty contracts ended. The crate and the execution are familiar to Shadow. He hears the cat's new meow, the blade begins to hum, and the story ends on **To Be Continued…**

**Panel 1 — Act II // cargo**

The power station's underground warehouse. Forty contracts have ended in this room. The target sits under the same lamp.

**Panel 2 — Target Confirmed**

No ID. No warning. Just a damp wooden crate that he already knows by heart.

**Panel 3 — Execute Contract**

Shadow draws his blade. One strike. He has done this so many times he could do it asleep.

**Panel 4 — silent beat**

- **......:** Meow.

**Panel 5 — Cargo**

The lid slowly springs open. The thing inside is trembling. The blade in his hand starts to hum.

**End card:** To Be Continued…

## Continuity notes for the next chapter

- **Shadow** is a codename shared by the selectable Klint and Quel operatives.
- **Shadow-041** marks the forty-first loop; the previous forty runs ended with Nix's death and a reset.
- The Warden protects Nix and half-remembers each night. His unfinished sentence is a plea.
- Nix is the spirit bound to the sword, now in a cat's body. The blade humming in the final panel recognizes her.
- The client **N.** remains unidentified. The story deliberately leaves open why Shadow hesitates this time; the meow may be new.
- Main dialogue lives in `data/story.xml`; fallback comic captions live in `scripts/post_boss_cinematic.gd`. Gameplay cards live in `scripts/tutorial.gd` and `scripts/boss_battle.gd`.
